“What can I make with what I've got?”

Gather turns your pantry, available time, and preferences into a dinner plan. Walk through a recipe, use a cooking timer, and take only the missing ingredients to the store.

Tell Gather→Call MCP tools→Make dinner

Real tools. Visible decisions.

The planner, recipe lookup, and shopping list call the local MCP server. Open the tool activity below the conversation to inspect requests and responses.

A deliberately small kitchen.

Six curated recipes and a deterministic command parser power this demo. It doesn't call a language model. Ingredient matches check presence, not available quantities. No nutrition or allergen-safety guarantees.

Your browser is your pantry.

Pantry entries, preferences, and shopping checks stay in local storage on this device. Planning sends ingredient names and preferences to the server running this app. Reset demo clears Gather's saved state.

Made for an Alexa+ future.

This simulator is not connected to an Alexa device or Amazon account. A live Alexa+ integration needs separate registration, hosting, and testing. Optional browser voice features are separate from Alexa.
